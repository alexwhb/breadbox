Handheld DMM

Clip the probes onto any two nodes on the bench. The meter behaves like the real thing: it loads the circuit with its input impedance, auto-ranges with a settling delay, shows last-digit flicker from noise, and reads true-RMS on AC.

Display6000 counts
DC V accuracy±(0.05% + 1)
Input impedance10 MΩ ∥ 100 pF
Burden (10 A)0.03 V/A
Probing A0 (J1 out): meter load shifts the node by −0.4 mV — shown as it would be on a real bench.
